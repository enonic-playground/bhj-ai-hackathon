### Implementation contract

1. **Word bank.** Bundle at least 50 curated, unique common English words of 4–8 ASCII letters, with nonempty categories; include repeated-letter words. Initial progression is exactly 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 letters for levels 1–5, so provide at least ten entries per length. Validate normalized uniqueness, allowed characters, categories and pool sufficiency at load/build. Choose randomly from eligible unused entries using injected randomness and track used words per run. Death and wrong guesses retain the selected word; only a new level selects another. Restart/replay clears the used set. No external word service. Invalid/insufficient custom test content must fail clearly rather than loop or silently repeat. Existing APPLE fixture journeys may pin a word independently of normal level length in the fixture build only; document that distinction.
2. **Campaign transitions.** Start a fresh three-life, zero-score run at level one. Solving levels 1–4 awards the word bonus once and opens LEVEL_COMPLETE; only a deliberate Next level action advances exactly once. Show solved word, level, bonus, total score and lives. Level five ends in CAMPAIGN_COMPLETE with final score, best score and replay/title actions; never create level six. GAME_OVER still reveals the current word and permits a fresh restart/title. Terminal/menu states freeze simulation and ignore gameplay keys. Rapid clicks/key repeat cannot skip levels, duplicate bonuses or restart accidentally. Preserve score, lives and the extra-life-earned flag across levels; reset word/guesses, dots/pellets, player, ball, enemies, phase/release/frightened/protection timers, pending input, fruit and fruit thresholds. Fresh level actors use their normal starts and release schedule, with no inherited protection; death still grants M3's two seconds. Next level clears stale input and rebases frame timing before play.
3. **Difficulty.** Keep the authored maze and player/ball rules. Put five immutable level definitions in configuration; start enemy roaming speed factors at 0.75, 0.78, 0.81, 0.84 and 0.87 of player speed. Keep frightened speed, duration, release offsets and return speed at M3 defaults initially; ball stays 0.8. Recompute the movement/contact bound from the effective level settings. Death uses the current level's configuration, not level one's; restart resets level/difficulty. Record reason and evidence for tuning changes rather than silently changing gameplay contracts. Avoid an accidental global mutation that affects an existing run or another test.
4. **Fruit.** Trigger at `ceil(0.30 * originalNormalDotCount)` and `ceil(0.70 * originalNormalDotCount)` normal dots consumed in each level; denominator excludes pellets and never shrinks with remaining dots. Each threshold fires once per level, independent of deaths. Use one validated fixed reachable corridor for the fruit (the current player-start tile is a suitable initial choice), a distinct visible shape, and a ten-active-CHASE-second lifetime. Fruit awards `100 * level` once on collection and does not consume a dot/pellet occupying the same tile. Collection uses the existing reached-tile-centre convention; a fruit spawned under a player exactly at that centre may be collected that substep. At most one fruit is visible: if another threshold fires while it exists, queue the second fruit and spawn it at the next CHASE substep after collection/expiry, never overwrite the first or collect two instances in one substep. Freeze fruit lifetime and pending spawn processing outside CHASE. Expired fruit cannot be collected. Death removes active/pending fruit and resets its timer, but preserves threshold-fired flags and dot progress so no threshold can be farmed; later untriggered thresholds still work. A new level resets both thresholds. Document and test timer-boundary ordering with the existing collectibles → enemies → ball contract.
5. **Central scoring and extra life.** Retain dot 10, pellet 50, frightened-enemy chain 200/400/800/1600 capped, letter 100 per newly revealed position and word bonus 1,000; add fruit. Route all awards through one authority which, when score first crosses/reaches 10,000, grants one extra life and marks it earned for the run. This applies to every award source, including a word bonus ending level five, and can occur only once across all levels/deaths. Preserve the flag on next level and death; clear it on restart. For a collectible that crosses the threshold on a lethal-contact substep, award the extra life before subtracting the death life, matching PRD contact order. Provide visible/non-color-only extra-life feedback. Do not add deductions for misses or resetting a level, or a new life cap that discards the earned life.
6. **High score and storage.** Persist only a versioned validated nonnegative safe-integer best score for now. Show best on title and end summaries, keep it across fresh runs/reloads, and never overwrite a higher local best with a lower run score. Treat absent, malformed, negative, non-finite, unsupported-version or otherwise invalid data as no saved best. Handle storage access/read/write exceptions, including denied/quota-exceeded access, without crashing or stopping play; the session's in-memory best must still work. A reload always starts at title, never restores a partial run. No unused audio preferences or arbitrary new controls. ### Summary

The word bank, campaign transitions, fruit and scoring are each one small, testable piece added to the existing M1–M3 architecture rather than a parallel system: `Game` still owns one private transition per state change, one movement engine, and one simulation clock.

`src/game/words.ts` gains `WORD_BANK` — 50 curated, categorized words, at least ten per length 4–8 — validated at module load by `validateWordBank` (uniqueness, per-length pool size), and `selectWordForLevel`, which draws uniformly from the entries of a requested length excluding an injected set. `src/game/levels.ts`'s `LEVELS` fixes each level's word length and roaming enemy-speed factor. `Game` now tracks a per-run `#usedWords` set and an `#effectiveConfig` (the base `config` with only `enemySpeedFactor` overridden for the current level), both rebuilt by `#resetLevelState`, the one reset shared by a fresh run (`startLevel`) and a level transition (`nextLevel`); only `startLevel` additionally resets score, lives, the used-word set and the earned-extra-life flag, which is what lets `nextLevel` preserve them. Solving level five's word skips `level-complete` and enters `campaign-complete` directly, in the same `#completeRound` that already guarded the word bonus against a repeat award.

`src/game/fruit.ts` holds the fruit's shape and the pure threshold arithmetic. `Game` spawns fruit on the maze's own spawn tile, computes both dots-consumed thresholds once from the maze's fixed dot count, and tracks one optional `#fruit` plus a `#pendingFruitSpawn` flag: a threshold crossed with none up spawns at once, a threshold crossed while one is up only queues the next, and every substep checks that queue before its own timers and movement, so a freed slot is filled only on the substep that follows the one that freed it. Death clears an active or queued fruit but not the fired-threshold flags or the dots-consumed count, so a spent threshold cannot be farmed by dying next to it.

Every score award — dots, pellets, a letter, the word bonus, an eaten enemy, fruit — now runs through one private `#awardScore`, which grants the run's sole extra life the instant score first reaches `config.extraLifeScoreThreshold`. Because collectibles already resolve before enemy contacts in the existing per-slice order, a collectible that crosses the threshold on a lethal-contact slice is granted before that slice's death takes a life, with no new ordering code.

`src/app/bestScore.ts`'s `BestScoreStore` is the one piece of M4 state that lives outside the DOM-free simulation, since it is a storage concern: it takes an injectable `StorageLike`, treats every absent, malformed or write-denied case as no saved best, and never regresses. `resolveLocalStorage` probes a real round trip before trusting `window.localStorage`. The shell records a score only when it changes and shows `best` on the title screen and both end panels; a reload always returns to the title screen.

The shell, `index.html` and the renderer gained the level `N/5` HUD, a static (non-flashing) extra-life badge and one-time announcement, a fruit shape, a Next level action on the level-complete panel, and a new campaign-complete panel with the final and best score. `src/app/fixture.ts` gained `testWords=<i1>,<i2>,...`, which pins level 1, 2, 3… to `SEED_WORDS` entries in order, alongside the unchanged `testWord=<index>` the M2/M3 single-round journeys already used. Design choices and their reasons are recorded as [D018](../DECISIONS.md#d018--m4-implementation-choices).
