Hac-Man

Chase through the maze and collect dots. Catching a ball to guess letters arrives in the next milestone.

How to play

  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD.
  • On touch, use the on-screen directional pad.
  • A turn is queued and taken at the first junction that allows it.
  • Each dot scores 10 points. Use the side tunnel to cross the maze.
