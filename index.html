Hac-Man

Chase the ball through the maze. Catch it to guess a letter of the hidden word; a wrong letter sends you back into the maze.

How to play

  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD; on touch, use the on-screen pad.
  • A turn is queued and taken at the first junction that allows it.
  • Catch the ringed ball to freeze the maze and guess a letter.
  • A correct letter reveals every copy and scores 100 a position; a wrong one costs you another chase.
  • Each dot scores 10 points. Solving the word scores 1,000 and ends the round.
