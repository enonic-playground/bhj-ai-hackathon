Hac-Man

Chase the ball through the maze while four enemies chase you. Catch it to guess a letter of the hidden word; a wrong letter sends you back into the maze.

Best score: 0

How to play

  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD; on touch, use the on-screen pad. Escape or the Pause button pauses.
  • A turn is queued and taken at the first junction that allows it.
  • Catch the ringed ball — its colour drifts through the spectrum — to freeze the maze and guess a letter.
  • A correct letter reveals every copy and scores 100 a position; a wrong one costs you another chase.
  • Touching Chaser, Ambusher, Patroller or Prowler costs one of your three lives.
  • Eat a pulsing power pellet to turn them blue for six seconds: 200, 400, 800 then 1,600 points each.
  • Each dot scores 10 points and a pellet 50. Solving the word scores 1,000 and advances the level.
  • Fruit appears twice a level, worth 100 times the level number; reach 10,000 points for one extra life.
  • Five levels of climbing word length end the campaign; your best score is kept on this device.
